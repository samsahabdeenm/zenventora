Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Does every website cost ₹1,999?

No. ₹1,999 is the starting price for an eligible basic scope. Final price depends on pages, design, content, functionality, integrations and support.

Can you build e-commerce websites?

Yes. E-commerce can include product catalogues, categories, cart and checkout, payments and delivery integrations based on the agreed requirements and quote.

Can you build portfolio, business, landing page and publication websites?

Yes. We can scope business, portfolio, campaign landing page, blog, news/publication, e-commerce and custom websites.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

We can include responsive layouts, semantic structure, metadata, crawlable content and other SEO foundations within the agreed scope. Rankings are not guaranteed.

Can you help with AEO and AI search visibility?

We can use clear answers, structured information, relevant schema, FAQs and technical SEO practices to help search and AI systems understand your site. No ranking, recommendation or citation can be guaranteed.

Can you maintain the website after launch?

Yes. Maintenance, content updates, SEO and enhancements can be scoped separately depending on your ongoing needs.
