Online Solutions FAQ

Questions customers ask before starting.

How much does Zenventora website work cost?

The online setup is requirement-based. The final scope depends on the website and the additional Google, platform, SEO, digital marketing or support requirements.

Can Zenventora build the complete online presence for a startup?

Yes. We can help a startup move from an idea or early requirement toward a practical online presence, then expand the digital setup as the business develops.

Do you provide online solutions for real estate, retail, mobile shops, cafés and restaurants?

Yes. These are current focus verticals, with page structures and customer journeys adapted to each business type.

Can you build a website for a business outside these verticals?

Yes. The verticals are focus areas rather than limits. We can discuss websites for service businesses, professional services, education, healthcare, local brands and other requirements.

Can Zenventora help with Google visibility and SEO?

Yes. Website projects can include strong SEO foundations such as crawlable content, useful headings, internal links, metadata, canonical URLs, structured data and customer-focused FAQs. Specific rankings cannot be guaranteed.

Can Zenventora help with AI search and LLM discovery?

We use clear content, structured information, useful answers, descriptive headings and technical SEO foundations to help search and AI-powered systems understand a business. No specific AI recommendation or answer can be guaranteed.

Can you help after the website launches?

Yes. Depending on the agreed scope, we can support maintenance, content updates, SEO, Google visibility, social media, digital marketing and future digital requirements.
